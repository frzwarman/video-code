# Arus — iPhone Shortcut tutorial · TREATMENT

9:16 · 1080×1920 · 60fps · ~78s with the stand-in voice · Bahasa Indonesia
Voice: generated by `npm run tts -- arus-shortcut` from `arus-shortcut/voice.json` (stand-in until Gemini's daily quota resets). Each scene lasts lead + its clip + tail; every cue is keyed to a voice segment in `public/vo/arus-shortcut/manifest.json`, so the ranges below move with the voice. Audio: voice + generated sound effects only.

## Look
Same world as videos 1 and 2 (see `arus/TREATMENT.md` → Look): the jade Home band as the backdrop, white panels, pop tiles, Plus Jakarta Sans, the 3D logo tile (`lib/Logo3D.tsx`) on the hook and the end card. One phone at a time, tight camera on the control the voice names, highlighter under the key word.
- **Arus screens** are redrawn from the app: Settings → the **iPhone quick add** card (`SettingsPage.tsx` → `QuickAddCard`), the Home band (`lib/Home.tsx`).
- **iOS screens** (the shared Shortcut's sheet and setup page, Back Tap settings, the list sheet, notifications) are redrawn in a neutral iOS-like style: no Apple marks, no real app icons.
- **The bank receipt** is a neutral "Transfer Berhasil" screen with no bank's name or logo. The account it books to is the viewer's own **BCA** account in Arus, as in video 2.
- **Messages** are the app's real wording (English, as Arus sends them): `Spent Rp 152.500 · KEDAI KOPI · Makan · BCA`, `Already saved · Spent Rp 48.000 · KEDAI KOPI · Makan · BCA`.
- The token, apikey and URL are shown as placeholder values (`arus_…` dotted out), never real ones.

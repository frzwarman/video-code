## Look
Same world as the promo (see `arus/TREATMENT.md` → Look): the jade Home band as the backdrop, white panels, pop tiles, Plus Jakarta Sans, the app's own screens redrawn in code (`lib/Home.tsx` and the page mock-ups this video adds), one phone at a time, tight camera on the control the voice names, highlighter under the key word, spins on tiles as they land. Five features, one flow: set the app up in setup order.

## Motion (lib/motion.tsx)
- **One continuous take.** No fades or blur at cuts. S1→S2, S3→S4 and S6→S7 are scale-through cuts (the outgoing shot rushes in ×1.3 on an accelerating curve, the next lands from 1.7×) with the wave sweep; S2→S3, S4→S5 and S5→S6 are relays: the phone stays exactly where it is, only its screen changes, and the camera carries on from the last shot. S2–S3 are on the iPhone, S4–S6 on the Android phone.
- **Camera:** zoom eased in log space on a camera curve, move length set by distance, every hold creeping in slowly; four hard punch-ins (2–2.4×) on the control the voice names: the Bank account chip (S2), the dumbbell (S3), Copy last month (S4), the crop square (S6).
- **Taps:** the control itself squishes and recoils, with a crisp focus ring; no expanding rings. Lock-ins pulse with a real attack. No bounce or spin-ins; new rows open their slot and push the list.
- **Type:** the newest typed character arrives in the accent; captions and the end card reveal word by word.

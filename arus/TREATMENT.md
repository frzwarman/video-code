# Arus — promo · TREATMENT

9:16 · 1080×1920 · 60fps · ~111s · Bahasa Indonesia
Voice: generated by `npm run tts` from `arus/voice.json` (Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, voice Kore, one request per group of scenes, split at the pauses; the Mac voice stands in for anything Gemini could not do). Each scene lasts lead + its clip + tail, and every cue is keyed to a voice segment in `public/vo/manifest.json`, so the ranges below move with the voice. Audio: voice + generated sound effects only.

## Look
- Background: the app's Home band, jade (`oklch(0.47 0.115 163) → oklch(0.55 0.14 161)`) with a mint glow, drawn once behind every scene. Faint rising wave lines (the logo's wave, repeated) hold still within a scene and glide one half-step at each cut. No drifting particles, no floating blobs.
- Cards are the app's `panel`: solid white, hairline border, ink-hued shadow, 24 px radius at app scale (48 px at film scale); icons sit in pop tiles. Text directly on the band is white; emphasis on the band is mint (`highlight`). OS chrome (iOS notification) stays dark; sheets, menus and browser bars are light, as on a phone in light mode.
- Icon stickers (white tiles) pop in where a scene needs context: S1 expenses (coffee, bag, bus, receipt, ticket) on the ticks, S3 coffee with the notification, S6 calendar by the day counter.
- The app's own colours after the revamp (src/index.css, light theme): ink on white panels, accent jade, income green, expense red, transfer blue, warn ochre, and the nine "pop" tile tones (mint, sky, sun, coral, grape, bubblegum, tangerine, ocean, slate).
- Type: **Plus Jakarta Sans** everywhere; weight carries the hierarchy (800 display, 700 headings, 600 labels), tabular figures for rupiah.
- Motif: the logo's white wave line = *arus* (flow). It draws on to move between scenes and carries money in S7. Every motion starts and stops on a beat; nothing idles.
- Safe zone: no text in the top 220px or bottom 380px (Reels/TikTok UI).

## Motion
Everything moves for a reason: it shows money moving, text being read, or data travelling. Nothing idles.
- The backdrop's currents drift steadily (a current never stops) and glide an extra half-step at each cut.
- Most scenes open tight and pull out to reveal (a **zoom-out**), then zoom in on the beat the voice names. Icons, badges and stickers **spin** into place as they land. The key word of each line gets a **highlighter** stroke drawn under it, left to right, as the voice says it (mint on the band, yellow on white panels).
- Every scene pushes in slowly (scale 1 → 1.035 across its length), and has a **camera**: `export const camera` lists zoom beats (frame, zoom, focus point) that the shell eases between; it zooms into what the voice names (a notification, a receipt, a lock, a tapped button) and pulls back for the wide view. Cuts are a quick blur-and-scale: the outgoing scene drops to a quarter opacity over 8 frames, the incoming one settles from blur over 10, and the wave sweep rides the cut.
- Shared assets (`Fx.tsx`): **ScanBeam** (a light bar reading the screen), **FlowDots** (beads streaming along a path), **FlyChip** (a pill flying on an arc), **Ring** (a pulse from a point), **scramble** (seeded cipher text); **kick** (a decaying flinch) in `core.ts`.
- Counting figures round to whole thousands.
- S1: each tick sends a red "−Rp …" chip from the balance to the sticker it paid for; the words blur in.
- S2: the bank card tilts back in 3D on "Arus"; beads flow down each branch; amounts count up.
- S3: the phone wobbles on each tap; a scan beam reads the receipt; the category list slides up and the picked row lights; the check in "Langsung tercatat." draws on.
- S4: the wordmark letters stagger in; the headline builds word by word; cards tilt up from the bottom with icons rotating in and amounts counting; a total line (Rp 947.000) draws at the end.
- S5: three payment screens slide in; a scan beam reads them all; the three fields fly on arcs from the receipt into the transaction row, shrinking and recolouring as they land; a ring pulses when the category snaps on, and again when the "Diingat" tag lands.
- S6: bars have a bright head while the month runs; the day counter ticks; Hiburan flinches and gets an amber "%" badge when it crosses 85%.
- S7: the real dashboard in a phone; stats count, bars and donut draw; camera: Income → Expenses → scroll → transfer row.
- S8: the rows slide into each phone; a check lands on each phone as the number settles; a ring pulses on each number as the line passes.
- S9: the lock snaps shut with a ring; the title blurs in.
- S10: the dashes march toward the middle until the cut; the X spins in; the bank dims; each chip flinches as it is struck.
- S11: a scan beam reads the screenshot; the text lines fly to an "Arus" pill at the top that pulses as each arrives; then the image dissolves: never saved, never sent.
- S12: a packet leaves the phone, passes through the first lock and comes out as scrambled cipher text, then lands in the database as the second lock closes.
- S13: cards tilt in; the eye's slash draws on; three tracker chips (Analitik · Iklan · Pelacak) are struck through; under the second card the three invited people pop in and a fourth, uninvited "?" is crossed out.
- S14: shot as close-ups (1.5–2.4×): the phone, the struck store chip, the address pill, then each thing the finger touches; tap rings on Share and on the row; the sheet slides up and drops; the browser view crossfades into the home screen and the icon pops in with a ring.
- S15: the logo's wave is drawn large behind the tile; the wordmark letters stagger; the link pulses twice with rings.
- App screens are the real post-revamp Home redrawn in code (`arus/Home.tsx`: jade band with the wave mark, wallet card with In/Out tiles and quick actions, shortcut tiles, insight cards, Recent activity, Where it went, Budgets, the tab bar around the Add button), hardcoded with the video's figures; the Accounts cards use the Accounts-page look (cash sun, bank ocean, e-wallet grape).
